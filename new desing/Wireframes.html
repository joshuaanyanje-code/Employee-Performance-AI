Team Intelligence — Wireframes

Information architecture, navigation patterns, and primary actions for an EPS workforce/HR app. Five authenticated roles plus an unauthenticated kiosk mode. Currency context: Kenya (KES, M-Pesa). Output is mid-fi: gray boxes, real placeholder copy from spec, light annotations. No final branding.

roles: master · superadmin · admin · hr · employee kiosk: ?kiosk=<branch>&org=<org> session: ?auth=<token> currency: KES · M-Pesa
